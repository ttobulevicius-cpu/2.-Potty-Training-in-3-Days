Part 3 of Guide

The 3-day roadmap at a glance

Each day builds on the one before. Your job: watch closely, stay positive, and keep going.

1

Day 1: Learn the Feeling

Diapers go away completely. Bare bottom or undies only. Lots of hydration, prompting every 15–20 minutes, and catching streams mid-way.

Day 1 Goal: Pee in the potty at least once (even halfway). That's a win!
2

Day 2: Fewer Reminders

Loose pants go on with no diapers underneath. Stretch reminders to every 30–45 mins. Try your first 15-min walk outside right after a success.

Day 2 Goal: More successes than accidents, and at least 1 self-initiation.
3

Day 3: Child Takes the Lead

Confidence day. Step back and let them tell you. Practice wiping, flushing, washing hands, and a 30–60 min outing to the park or store.

Day 3 Goal: Consistent bathroom trips away from home.
Part 4 • Potty Scripts

What to say (and what to avoid)

Kids who feel pushed tend to push back. Short, cheerful, matter-of-fact phrases work best.

Instead of saying... Try saying this...
✕ "Do you need to go potty?"
✓ "It's potty time!" (Make it a statement)
✕ "Yuck! You're so stinky!"
✓ "Let's get you clean and dry."
✕ "Why did you do that?!"
✓ "Oops! Next time, pee goes in the potty."
✕ "Big kids don't wet their pants."
✓ "You're learning. You'll get it."

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I receive the guide after buying? +
Right after checkout you'll get a download link on screen and by email. Read the PDF on your phone, tablet, or computer, or print it at home.
Does this mean my child will be dry at night too? +
Not necessarily — daytime and nighttime dryness develop separately, and night dryness often comes later. This plan focuses on daytime independence, and the guide covers how to handle naps and nights in the meantime.
What if my toddler refuses to sit on the potty? +
Part 7 (Troubleshooting) covers this: offering choices ("Big potty or little potty?"), a one-song timer, and letting a favorite stuffed animal "try first."
What if 3 days isn't enough for my child? +
Every child is different. Page 13 includes a "When to pause" guide — taking a 2–4 week break isn't failing, it's good timing. And if the guide doesn't help, email us within 30 days of purchase for a full refund.
